# Couple Pricing Model

## Status

This is the authoritative couple-facing pricing model for Zania as of July 22, 2026.

## Product principle

Couples do not pay to explore Zania or use its current wedding-planning tools. Payment begins when they want their vendors or planner to collaborate inside the wedding workspace.

The upgrade message must focus on collaboration, not guest count, wedding size, or fear of losing existing work.

## Intimate

Price: Free.

Intimate is for a couple exploring Zania and planning privately. It includes all current couple-facing planning features, including budgeting, tasks, timeline, guests and RSVPs, vendor and planner discovery, AI planning support, exports, and Gift Registry.

Intimate does not allow vendors or a planner to join and collaborate inside the wedding workspace. Existing free-tier wedding and device guardrails remain separate abuse-prevention controls.

## Collaborative

Price:

- KES 1,999 per month
- KES 15,000 per year

Collaborative includes everything in Intimate and adds:

- vendor collaboration inside the wedding workspace
- planner collaboration inside the wedding workspace
- shared coordination with those collaborators
- access to new Collaborative features as they are released

Future unreleased capabilities should be labelled `Coming soon`. They must not be represented as available before they are implemented.

## Billing rules

- The wedding workspace is the billable unit.
- Monthly access expires one month after successful payment unless renewed.
- Annual access expires one year after successful payment unless renewed.
- There are no couple-facing add-ons.
- Gift Registry and RSVP management are not separately billable.
- Legacy Basic, Premium, Planning Pass, Gift Registry add-on, and RSVP add-on lookup keys must not be offered for new couple checkouts.

## Upgrade copy

Preferred message:

> Ready to plan together? Upgrade to Collaborative to invite your vendors and planner into your Zania wedding workspace.
